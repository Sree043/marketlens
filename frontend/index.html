Please read

Market data comes from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint. Covering every NSE stock does not mean every stock has a fresh quote: illiquid shares may not have traded for hours or days, and the provider may throttle requests. Each quote shows its age and is marked Delayed, Stale or Unavailable rather than being presented as live. Signals are simple technical rules, not validated predictions or advice. Paper orders use virtual money only and ignore brokerage, taxes and slippage.
